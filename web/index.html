SH—01 / GET CONNECTED

A little setup.
A lot of possibility.

The browser canvas works on its own. Add T3 for live sessions, then a device for voice.

  1. Explore the surface

    Try an ambient program, a chart, or a message. For sample sessions without connecting T3, run:

    python3 bridge.py --demo

  2. Bring your sessions in

    Start T3 Code on this computer. Run the bridge without demo mode, then refresh this page:

    python3 bridge.py

    Custom database? Add --database /path/to/state.sqlite. The bridge reads session status without changing the database.

  3. Give your desk a voice

    Connect the supported round display and follow the repository’s device and voice setup. Once the companion is running, choose a session under Voice and enable input.

    The microphone is on the device. Hold its glass to talk. The voice panel shows whether release sends immediately or opens a review.
